🦋 My Journey

Every chapter shaped the person I am today. This journey is still being written.

🌱 Beginning

A Curious Child

Curiosity has always been my favorite companion. From an early age, I loved asking questions, exploring new ideas, and understanding how things worked. Whether it was discovering something in a book or learning through everyday experiences, I enjoyed finding meaning in the little details. Looking back, that curiosity became the foundation of the person I am today.

🩺 Chapter 2

Pre-Med Journey

My academic journey first began in the world of Biology and Pre-Medical studies. Those years taught me discipline, patience, observation, and resilience. Although my path eventually changed, the analytical mindset and strong work ethic I developed during that time continue to influence how I approach every challenge and every new opportunity.

💻 Chapter 3

Discovering Computer Science

Choosing Computer Science was one of the most meaningful decisions I've made. It introduced me to a world where creativity and logic work together to solve real-world problems. As I explored web development and design, I realized technology is not only about writing code—it's about creating experiences that make people's lives easier, more engaging, and more meaningful.

✨ Today

Learning Every Day

Today, I'm constantly learning through university, internships, and personal projects. Alongside frontend development, I've developed a growing interest in Human Resources, technology management, and understanding how people and technology can work together. Every new experience teaches me something valuable and motivates me to keep improving both personally and professionally.

🚀 Future

What's Next?

My journey is still unfolding, and I look forward to every chapter ahead. I hope to build meaningful digital experiences, contribute to people-centered technology, and continue growing in areas such as Human Resources, Technology, Design, and Data. More than anything, I want to keep learning, stay curious, and create work that makes a positive impact.
